## 🚨 CRITICAL SUCCESS FACTORS

### 1. **Packaging = Premium Pricing**
- Don't ship in plain poly bags
- Use branded packaging, hang tags, retail-ready presentation
- Invest in custom packaging for 2x price increase

### 2. **Retail-Specific Designs**
- NOT just Etsy bestsellers
- Think: gift shops, not online marketplaces
- Family-friendly (retail is less edgy than online)

### 3. **MOQ (Minimum Order Quantity)**
- Start with 12-24 units per design
- Retailers want to TEST before committing
- Low MOQ = easier to get first orders

### 4. **Net-60 Payment Terms**
- Retailers expect 60-day payment terms
- Use **Faire.com** (they front the money, you get paid in 7 days!)
- Factor this into cash flow planning

### 5. **Seasonal Timing**
- Pitch Christmas products in JUNE/JULY
- Pitch Valentine's in OCTOBER/NOVEMBER
- Retailers buy 4-6 months ahead
