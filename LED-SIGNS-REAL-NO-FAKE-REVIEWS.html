How long do LED signs last?

Our LED signs last 50,000-100,000 hours. That's about 10-15 years if you run them 8 hours a day. They're built tough and come with a warranty for peace of mind.

Can I use LED signs outdoors?

Yes! Many of our LED signs are weather-resistant and perfect for outdoor use. They handle rain, snow, and temperature changes like a champ. Just look for "outdoor rated" in the description.

Prices vary based on size and design, but most custom LED signs range from $150 to $800. Simple open signs start around $50. Contact us for a free quote on your specific needs.
