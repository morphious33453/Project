Frequently Asked Questions

Does it work through tinted windows?

Yes! NFC technology works through glass (up to 5mm thick) including tinted, privacy, and ceramic-tinted windows. The darker the tint, the more discreet your NFC sticker placement. The decorative "TAP HERE" decal goes on the outside where people can see it.

Can I change the destination URL after it's programmed?

Absolutely. Every sticker comes with a free cloud dashboard login. Change your link anytime - switch from Instagram to TikTok, promote different events, update menu links. No reprogramming needed. Changes are instant.

Will it damage my car window or paint?

No. The NFC sticker uses 3M adhesive designed for glass surfaces. It leaves no residue when removed. The decorative "TAP HERE" decal is vinyl with removable adhesive. Both are safe for automotive glass and can be repositioned during installation.

Do people need a special app to tap?

No app required. Modern iPhones (iPhone XS and newer with iOS 14+) and Android phones (NFC-enabled, most made after 2018) read NFC tags automatically. Users just hold their phone near the sticker and a notification pops up with your link.

How weatherproof is it really?

These stickers are rated IP68 (fully waterproof and dustproof) and tested from -25°C to +70°C. They survive Ontario winters, summer heat, car washes, and rain. We back them with a 2-year warranty. If they fail, we replace them free.

Can I track how many people tap my sticker?

Yes. Your dashboard shows: total taps, taps per day/week/month, geographic data (city-level), device type (iOS vs Android), and peak tap times. Great for measuring ROI and optimizing placement.

Where's the best place to put it on my car?

Most popular: rear side windows (visible at red lights), rear windshield (lower corner), side windows where people walk by in parking lots. Avoid driver/passenger front windows (obstructs view). Include installation guide with every order.

What if someone steals or damages my car with the sticker?

The sticker only provides the link YOU program - typically a public social media profile you already share publicly. It doesn't give access to your car, personal data, or location. It's no different from having your Instagram handle printed on your car.
