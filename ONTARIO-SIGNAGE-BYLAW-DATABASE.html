🎯 Quick Reference: Most Common Questions

Do I Need a Permit for My Business Sign in Ontario?

Generally YES if:

  • Sign area exceeds 2 square feet (0.19 m²)
  • Sign is permanently affixed to building or ground
  • Sign is illuminated or projects over property line
  • Sign is on a heritage property

Generally NO PERMIT needed for:

  • Window decals under 25% of window area
  • Temporary real estate signs (specific size limits apply)
  • Construction site notice boards
  • Temporary event signs (7-14 day limit)

⚠️ IMPORTANT: Regulations vary significantly by municipality. Always verify with your local municipality before installing signage.
