## 📞 GETTING YOUR GOOGLE REVIEW LINK

### Method 1: From Google Business Profile
1. Log into Google Business Profile
2. Click "Home"
3. Click "Get more reviews"
4. Copy the short link shown
5. Use the part after `/r/` before `/review`

**Example link:**
`https://g.page/r/CZkj8xKT-28BEAE/review`

Extract: `CZkj8xKT-28BEAE`

Replace in HTML:
```html
<a href="https://g.page/r/CZkj8xKT-28BEAE/review"
```

### Method 2: From Your Public Profile
1. Google your business name
2. Click "Reviews" in knowledge panel
3. Copy the URL
4. Use that full URL in the link
