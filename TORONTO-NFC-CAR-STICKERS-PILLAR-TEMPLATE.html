Do NFC car stickers work with all phones at Toronto meets?

Yes. Modern iOS (iPhone XS+, iOS 14+) and Android phones (2018+) support automatic NFC reading—no app needed. 72% of Canadian smartphones are NFC-enabled. Our hybrid design includes a QR code backup for older devices, ensuring 100% compatibility at Downsview meets and lakeshore cruises.

Are these safe on tinted or defrost-lined rear glass?

Yes. NFC works through glass up to 5mm thick, including factory tint, aftermarket ceramic tint, and privacy film. Apply to the inside of rear glass, avoiding defrost lines when possible. Outdoor-grade vinyl + encased NFC chip handle Toronto winters (-40°C tested). Avoid pressure washing for 24 hours after install.

Will they survive Toronto winters and car washes?

Yes. Weatherproof laminate and encased NFC tested from -40°C to +80°C. Survives slush, salt, automatic car washes, and lakeshore humidity. We back all stickers with a 2-year warranty. Wipe clean before meets for best tap performance.

What happens if I sell my car or switch builds?

Order a replacement sticker and reapply to your new build. Your NFC link and dashboard stay the same—no need to update followers. Replacement stickers available at discounted rates for existing customers.

Can I link to Linktree instead of direct Instagram?

Yes. Program any URL: Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Linktree, booking page, merch shop, OnlyFans, Patreon, or custom landing page. Most Toronto creators use Linktree to consolidate all platforms into one tap.

Where should I place the NFC sticker for maximum taps at Downsview meets?

Best placement based on Toronto creator data: Rear window center, lower third (89% tap rate). This position is visible to people walking behind your car at meets and to drivers at red lights. Avoid upper corners (blocked by headrests) and side windows (lower tap rate at parking lots).
