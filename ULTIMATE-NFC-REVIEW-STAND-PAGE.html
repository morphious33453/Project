Frequently Asked Questions

How does NFC technology work for reviews?
NFC (Near Field Communication) is the same technology used for contactless payments like Apple Pay. We embed an NFC tag in the stand that contains your Google review link. When customers tap their NFC-enabled smartphone on the stand, it reads the tag and automatically opens your review page in their browser. Works with iPhone XS/XR and newer, plus all Android phones from 2013 onward. No app download required — it's built into the phone's operating system.
What if my customers don't have NFC phones?
That's why we include a QR code! Any smartphone with a camera can scan QR codes (built into iPhone camera since iOS 11, Android since Android 9). If someone has an older phone without NFC, they simply point their camera at the QR code and tap the notification that appears. Both methods land them on the same Google review page. Dual technology ensures 99%+ smartphone compatibility.
Do I need to provide my Google review link?
Yes. When ordering, you'll provide your Google review link so we can pre-program both the NFC tag and QR code. To find your link: Search for your business on Google → Click "Reviews" → Click "Write a review" → Copy the URL from your browser address bar. It usually looks like "https://g.page/r/[YOUR-CODE]/review". Send this to us and we'll handle the rest. If you need help finding it, we provide step-by-step instructions.
Can I update the review link if my business moves?
The NFC tag can be reprogrammed using any NFC writing app (free on App Store and Google Play). We provide instructions on how to update it yourself if your Google review URL changes. The QR code sticker can be replaced — we'll send you a new one free if your link changes within the first year. Most businesses never need to change it, but the option exists if you relocate or change business names.

Power consumption is negligible compared to the value of the reviews you'll generate.

Set one up first (it's free). Go to google.com/business and claim your business listing. This is essential for local SEO anyway — customers search Google to find businesses like yours. Once your profile is verified (usually 1-2 weeks), you'll be able to get your review link.
