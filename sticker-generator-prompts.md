## 💡 PRO TIPS FOR STICKER GENERATION:

### Style Modifiers to Add:
- `die-cut style, no background, transparent background`
- `vector art, flat design, clean lines`
- `glossy sticker finish, vinyl texture`
- `white border around sticker, kiss-cut style`
- `waterproof vinyl appearance, durable material`
- `holographic effect, iridescent shine` (for premium stickers)

### Size Specifications:
- Small: `2-3 inch diameter, perfect for laptops and water bottles`
- Medium: `3-4 inch, standard sticker size`
- Large: `5-6 inch, car window decal size`
- Sheet: `8.5x11 inch sheet with multiple stickers`

### Color Optimization:
- `vibrant colors, high contrast, eye-catching`
- `pastel aesthetic, soft muted tones`
- `bold saturated colors, pop art style`
- `monochrome black and white, minimalist`

### Technical Specs:
- `300 DPI, print-ready quality`
- `CMYK color mode for printing`
- `vector format, scalable design`
- `contour cut line, die-cut ready`
