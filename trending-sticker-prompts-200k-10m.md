## 💡 PRO TIPS FOR MAXIMUM IMPACT

### Style Consistency:
- **Die-cut ready:** `no background, transparent background, die-cut style`
- **Print quality:** `300 DPI, print-ready, high resolution`
- **Vinyl aesthetic:** `glossy sticker finish, waterproof vinyl texture`
- **Size optimization:** `2-3 inch perfect for laptops and water bottles`

### Text Optimization:
- Use BOLD, READABLE fonts
- Text must be legible at 2-inch size
- Avoid thin script fonts (they don't print well small)
- High contrast: dark text on light background or vice versa

### Color Strategy:
- **Vibrant colors** for aesthetic/trendy stickers
- **Pastel colors** for gentle/supportive messages (mental health, sobriety)
- **Bold colors** for fitness/motivation
- **Earth tones** for plant mom
- **Cosmic colors** for manifestation/zodiac

### Keyword Optimization for Listings:
- Title = EXACT keyword match
- Example: "Pickleball Paddle Sticker - Funny Pickleball Gift - Waterproof Vinyl Decal"
- Include trend-specific terms in description
- Tag with community hashtags
