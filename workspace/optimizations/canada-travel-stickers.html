How to Apply Canada Travel Stickers: Professional Installation Guide

Proper application ensures your travel stickers last their full 3-10 year lifespan on RVs, vehicles, laptops, and outdoor gear. Follow these professional steps for perfect results every time, even in Canadian weather conditions.

  1. Clean the Surface Thoroughly Use isopropyl alcohol (70%+) or glass cleaner to remove all dirt, dust, road grime, oils, and residue. For RVs/vehicles, wash first then wipe application area with alcohol. Dry completely with lint-free cloth. Clean surfaces ensure maximum adhesion.
  2. Check Temperature & Weather Surface temperature should be 50°F to 90°F (10°C to 32°C). If applying outdoors in Canadian winter, warm the surface first with a hair dryer or heat gun (low setting). Avoid applying in rain, snow, or when surface is wet/frosty. Room temperature applications work best.
  3. Plan Your Placement Before removing backing, hold sticker up to desired location. For RVs: popular spots are rear bumpers, doors, and slide-outs. For laptops: corners or centered. For water bottles: flat sides work best. Mark lightly with painter's tape if needed.
  4. Peel the Backing Paper Carefully peel away paper backing from vinyl sticker, exposing adhesive side. For larger stickers, peel slowly to prevent vinyl curling. Keep backing paper in case repositioning needed. Don't touch adhesive with fingers (oils reduce bonding).
  5. Position and Apply Starting from one edge or center, carefully position sticker on clean surface. Press down gently with fingers, working from center outward. For curved surfaces (water bottles, helmets), apply slowly and press as you go to prevent air bubbles.
  6. Smooth Out Air Bubbles Use credit card, squeegee, or soft cloth to firmly press sticker from center toward edges. This pushes out trapped air bubbles and ensures complete adhesion. Apply firm, even pressure. Small bubbles will disappear over 24-48 hours.
  7. Remove Transfer Tape (if applicable) If sticker has transfer tape on top, peel it away slowly at sharp angle after fully applying. If sticker starts lifting with tape, press back down and continue peeling more slowly. Most of our stickers don't require transfer tape.
  8. Final Pressing Use your squeegee or cloth to press entire sticker firmly one final time. Pay special attention to edges and corners to prevent premature peeling. For outdoor applications (RVs, vehicles), press edges extra firm.
  9. Allow Adhesive to Cure Wait 24-48 hours before exposing sticker to water, extreme temperatures, car washes, or harsh conditions. This allows adhesive to fully bond with surface for maximum durability. Avoid driving RVs at highway speeds for 24 hours if possible.
  10. Enjoy for Years Your Canada travel sticker is now professionally installed! It will last 3-10 years outdoors (RVs, vehicles) or indefinitely indoors (laptops, water bottles used indoors). Clean gently with mild soap and water when needed.
Canadian Winter Tip: If applying stickers to RVs or vehicles in winter, bring them inside overnight to warm to room temperature before application. Warm the vehicle surface with a hair dryer before applying. This ensures proper adhesive bonding in cold weather.
