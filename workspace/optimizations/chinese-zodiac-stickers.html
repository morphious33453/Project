How to Apply Your Chinese Zodiac Stickers: Step-by-Step Guide

Proper application ensures your zodiac stickers last their full 3-10 year lifespan outdoors or indefinitely indoors. Follow these professional installation steps for perfect results every time.

  1. Clean the Surface Thoroughly Use isopropyl alcohol (70% or higher) or glass cleaner to remove all dirt, dust, oils, and residue from the application surface. Dry completely with a lint-free cloth. Clean surfaces ensure maximum adhesion and prevent bubbling.
  2. Check Temperature Conditions Ensure the surface temperature is between 50°F and 90°F (10°C to 32°C). Avoid applying in extreme cold or heat, direct sunlight, or when surface is wet or frosty. Room temperature applications work best.
  3. Plan Your Placement Before removing any backing, hold the sticker up to your desired location and mark lightly with painter's tape or pencil if needed. Measure to ensure centering and alignment, especially for multiple stickers.
  4. Peel the Backing Paper Carefully peel away the paper backing from the vinyl sticker, exposing the adhesive side. For larger stickers, peel slowly to avoid the vinyl curling or sticking to itself. Keep the backing paper in case repositioning is needed.
  5. Position and Apply Starting from one edge or the center, carefully position the sticker on your clean surface. Press down gently with your fingers, working from the center outward to prevent air bubbles. Take your time for precise placement.
  6. Smooth Out Air Bubbles Use a credit card, squeegee, or soft cloth to firmly press the sticker from the center toward the edges. This pushes out any trapped air bubbles and ensures complete adhesion. Apply firm, even pressure.
  7. Remove Transfer Tape (if applicable) If your sticker comes with transfer tape on top, peel it away slowly at a sharp angle after the sticker is fully applied. If the sticker starts to lift with the tape, press back down and continue peeling more slowly.
  8. Final Pressing Once applied, use your squeegee or cloth to press the entire sticker firmly one final time. Pay special attention to edges and corners to ensure they're fully adhered. This prevents premature peeling.
  9. Allow Adhesive to Cure Wait 24-48 hours before exposing the sticker to water, extreme temperatures, or harsh conditions. This allows the adhesive to fully bond with the surface for maximum durability and longevity.
  10. Enjoy for Years Your Chinese zodiac sticker is now professionally installed! It will last 3-10 years outdoors or indefinitely indoors with proper care. Clean gently with mild soap and water when needed. No harsh chemicals or abrasive scrubbing.
Pro Tip: For outdoor applications on vehicles or windows, apply stickers to the inside surface when possible to protect from weather and extend lifespan to 10+ years. The design will show through clearly while the adhesive stays protected.

Your Chinese zodiac animal is determined by your birth year according to the Chinese lunar calendar. Our premium vinyl Chinese zodiac stickers last 3-10 years outdoors depending on environmental conditions. Yes and no. Our zodiac stickers can be removed from non-porous surfaces (glass, metal, plastic) without leaving residue, making them removable. ❓ What surfaces work best for Chinese zodiac stickers?

Chinese zodiac stickers adhere best to smooth, clean, non-porous surfaces including: glass windows and mirrors, painted walls (smooth finish), metal doors and appliances, plastic surfaces, laptops and electronics, vehicles (cars, bikes), acrylic and polycarbonate, and sealed wood.
