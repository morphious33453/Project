⚖️ Legal in Canada? Safety Concerns? Here's the Truth

Common questions about rear window decal legality and safety—answered with facts

✅ 100% Legal for Rear Windows

Transport Canada regulations allow rear window graphics because drivers rely on side mirrors for rear visibility, not rear windows. Commercial trucks often have no rear window at all (cargo vans, box trucks). As long as both side mirrors are functional and properly adjusted, rear window decals are fully legal across all provinces.

🚫 NOT Legal for Front/Side Windows

Important: These decals are designed ONLY for rear windows. Never apply to front windshield or front side windows—that's illegal in all provinces as it obstructs required driver visibility. Rear side windows have varying regulations by province.

👁️ Visibility from Inside

The 80/20 perforation ratio means 80% of the material is perforated holes, allowing excellent outward visibility from inside the cab. During the day, it's like looking through tinted windows. At night with interior lights off, visibility remains clear.

🌙 Night Visibility Considerations

If you turn on interior cab lights at night, the one-way effect reverses slightly (people outside can see silhouettes inside). This is true of all OWV films. Best practice: keep interior lights off while driving at night for optimal outward visibility.

🚔 Police & Inspections

Rear window decals are common and accepted by law enforcement across Canada. We've never heard of a customer receiving a ticket for properly installed rear window OWV graphics. Commercial vehicle inspectors focus on side mirror functionality, not rear window graphics.

📋 Insurance Considerations

Rear window decals don't typically affect insurance rates as they're cosmetic modifications. However, if you have a comprehensive custom vehicle policy, inform your insurer about modifications. Most standard policies don't require disclosure for decals.

🛠️ Professional Installation Guide

Follow these steps for bubble-free, professional-looking results. Installation takes 30-45 minutes with proper preparation.

1

Clean the Window

Critical step for adhesion. Use glass cleaner and microfiber cloth to thoroughly clean the outside of your rear window. Remove all dirt, dust, wax, and residues. Dry completely—any moisture will cause bubbles.

2

Measure & Position

Before removing backing, hold the decal up to your rear window to plan positioning. The 20×60" size fits most truck rear windows, but you may need to trim slightly for perfect fit. Mark position with tape if needed.

3

Temperature Check

Install when temperature is 10-30°C (50-86°F). If it's too cold, the adhesive won't bond properly. If it's too hot, the vinyl may stretch. For cold weather installation, warm the window with a heat gun first.

4

Peel & Stick Method

Peel back 2-3 inches of backing paper from the top edge. Align the top of the decal with the top of your window. Press firmly to adhere the top edge, ensuring it's straight and centered.

5

Squeegee Down

Slowly peel away more backing while using a squeegee or credit card to press the decal onto the glass. Work from top to bottom, pushing out air bubbles as you go. Use firm, overlapping strokes.

6

Eliminate Bubbles

If you see air bubbles, gently lift the vinyl near the bubble and re-squeegee. For stubborn bubbles, use a pin to poke a tiny hole and push the air out. Small bubbles often disappear naturally within 24-48 hours.

7

Trim Excess (If Needed)

If the decal overlaps your window edges slightly, use a sharp utility knife to trim excess material. Cut carefully along the window edge, using the rubber gasket as a guide.

8

Seal the Edges

Use a heat gun or hair dryer on low heat to warm the edges of the decal. This activates the adhesive for maximum bond strength. Press edges firmly with your fingers while warm.

9

Cure Time

Wait 24-48 hours before washing. The adhesive needs time to fully cure. Avoid driving through car washes or heavy rain during this period. Park in a garage if possible.

💡 Pro Tips for Best Results:

  • Use soapy water method: Spray window with very diluted soapy water before applying. This lets you reposition the decal before it permanently sticks. Squeegee out all water.
  • Two-person installation recommended: 60" width is easier to manage with a helper holding one side.
  • Avoid direct sunlight during install: Hot glass can cause vinyl to stretch. Install in shade or garage.
  • Keep it warm in winter: If installing in cold weather, store the decal roll indoors at room temperature for 24 hours before installation.
  • Check from inside: Periodically check from inside the cab to ensure you're not blocking your own visibility with bubbles or wrinkles.
