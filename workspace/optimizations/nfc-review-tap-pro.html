❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How does NFC technology work for review collection?

NFC (Near Field Communication) is the same technology used for Apple Pay and Google Pay. An NFC chip embedded in the stand contains your Google Review URL. When a customer taps their smartphone on the stand, the chip transmits the URL to the phone, which automatically opens the review page in their browser. No app downloads, no QR scanning, no typing required—it's truly effortless.

Will this work with my customers' phones?

Yes, with 90%+ of modern smartphones. iPhone 7 and newer (iOS 11+) and Android 4.0+ have NFC built-in. If your customers can use Apple Pay or Google Pay, they can tap your review stand. This covers virtually every phone from 2016 onwards.

Do customers need to download an app?

No! That's the beauty of NFC. It uses the phone's built-in NFC reader (same hardware for contactless payments). When they tap, their phone vibrates and the review page opens in their default browser automatically. Zero friction.

How is this better than QR codes?

NFC is instant (1 second) vs. QR codes requiring 5-15 seconds to open camera, focus, and scan. NFC works in any lighting—QR codes struggle in dim restaurants or bright sunlight. NFC has 40-60% completion rates vs. 15-25% for QR codes. The tactile "tap" interaction feels more engaging and trustworthy than pointing a camera.
