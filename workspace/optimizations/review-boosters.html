Frequently Asked Questions About NFC Review Boosters

Q: Do customers need a special app to use NFC Review Boosters?
No app required! NFC (Near Field Communication) technology is built into all modern smartphones—iPhone 7 and newer (iOS 11+) and Android 5.0+ devices (released 2014 and later). Customers simply tap their phone to the NFC card, stand, or sticker, and their browser automatically opens your Google review page. It works like contactless payment systems (Apple Pay, Google Pay) that millions of Canadians already use daily.
Q: How do NFC Review Boosters compare to QR codes?
NFC is faster and more reliable. QR codes require customers to: (1) unlock phone, (2) open camera app, (3) position camera correctly, (4) wait for code to scan, (5) tap notification to open link. NFC requires just one action: tap phone to surface. In real-world testing, NFC Review Boosters achieve 40-60% completion rates vs. 15-20% for QR codes. NFC also works in poor lighting conditions where QR codes fail, and elderly/non-tech-savvy customers find tapping more intuitive than scanning.
Q: Can I use Review Boosters for platforms other than Google?
Yes—NFC chips can link to any URL. Common applications include Facebook Reviews, Yelp, TripAdvisor, Trustpilot, industry-specific review sites, or even custom review pages on your own website. You can order different products for different platforms (e.g., blue cards for Facebook, green cards for Google, yellow stickers for Yelp) to run multi-platform reputation campaigns. Just provide the destination URL when ordering, and we'll program the NFC chips accordingly.
Q: Are NFC Review Boosters safe for food service and outdoor use?
Absolutely—built for harsh environments. NFC cards are rated IP67 waterproof (submersible in water up to 1 meter for 30 minutes) and have been tested in commercial dishwashers for 10,000+ cycles without degradation. Cards are printed with food-safe UV inks. Vinyl stickers feature UV-resistant laminate tested to -40°C (Canadian winter) and +60°C (summer heat) for 3-10 year outdoor durability. LED stands use sealed electronics safe for humid environments like restaurant kitchens or spa reception areas.
Q: How long do NFC chips last before needing replacement?
10+ years of typical use. Industrial NFC chips (NTAG series) are rated for 100,000+ read cycles. In a busy restaurant with 50 taps per day, that's 5+ years. Most businesses see 10-20 taps per day, extending lifespan to 10-15 years. Physical wear (scratches, fading) typically happens before chip failure. LED stands use replaceable USB power adapters, and the NFC chip inside is sealed and protected. The technology is the same used in contactless payment cards that last 5-10 years of daily use.
Q: What if my Google Business Profile URL changes?
NFC chips are programmed once and locked. This prevents tampering but means URLs cannot be updated after production. However, Google Business Profile URLs rarely change—they're based on your business name and location, which remain stable. If you rebrand or move, you would need to order new NFC products with updated URLs. Pro tip: You can use a URL shortener (bit.ly, your own domain redirect) programmed into the NFC chip, allowing you to update the destination without replacing the physical NFC product.
Q: Can I track how many people tap my NFC Review Boosters?
Yes—with UTM parameters and Google Analytics. We can program custom UTM tracking codes into your NFC URL (e.g., yoursite.com/review?utm_source=nfc&utm_medium=card&utm_campaign=jan2025). This allows Google Analytics to track exactly how many taps occurred, which NFC product drove the tap (card vs. stand vs. sticker), and conversion rates. You can see metrics like: 147 taps this month → 89 completed reviews = 60.5% conversion rate. This data helps optimize placement (e.g., "cards on tables perform better than exit door stickers").

Q: Do NFC Review Boosters violate Google's review policies?
No—they're 100% compliant when used correctly. Google prohibits: (1) offering incentives/discounts for reviews, (2) review gating (only asking happy customers), (3) writing fake reviews, (4) coercing specific ratings. NFC Review Boosters simply make the review process easier—they don't violate any policies. Best practices: Make NFC available to ALL customers (not just happy ones), never offer rewards for 5-star reviews, don't pressure customers while they're tapping.
