🏁 The Legend of the Screaming Chicken

The "screaming chicken" officially called the "Firebird Hood Decal" or "Phoenix Hood Bird" became an automotive icon when Pontiac introduced it on the 1973 Trans Am. The massive phoenix/firebird graphic spread across the hood symbolized power, performance, and rebellion during the muscle car era. Made famous by movies like Smokey and the Bandit (1977) starring Burt Reynolds' black and gold Trans Am, and TV's Knight Rider (1982-1986) with KITT, the screaming chicken transcended automotive decoration to become a cultural phenomenon.

Pontiac offered various phoenix designs through the years: the bold 1970s versions, the refined 1980s iterations, special editions like the 1980 Turbo Indy Pace Car with Solar Gold/Orange phoenix, and the final 2002 35th Anniversary models. Today, Trans Am and Firebird enthusiasts worldwide restore these classic muscle cars with authentic screaming chicken decals to preserve automotive history and celebrate an era when cars had personality.

Q: How long will this decal last on my car's hood?

Q: Is this the correct size for a 1977 Pontiac Trans Am hood?
24" decal is full hood size; 18" is scaled version. Original Trans Am hood decals varied by year: 1973-1974 models used ~70" wingspan designs, 1975-1981 used ~60" versions, 1982-1992 used smaller ~48" graphics. Our 24" (60cm) decal is a popular scaled size that fits most hoods proportionally. For period-correct full-size replica, order our 60"+ custom size and specify your year/model for accurate dimensions. Many restorers prefer slightly smaller decals (18"-24") for subtler look or daily drivers.
Q: Can I install this myself or do I need a professional?
Most enthusiasts can DIY with patience and proper technique. Hood decal installation requires: (1) Clean hood with isopropyl alcohol, (2) Spray soapy water on hood and decal adhesive, (3) Position decal centered on hood bulge, (4) Squeegee from center outward pushing out water and air bubbles, (5) Remove transfer tape slowly, (6) Final squeegee and edge pressing. Takes 30-60 minutes for first-timers. Professional installation ($50-$150) recommended for expensive show cars or nervous first-timers. YouTube has excellent Trans Am decal installation tutorials.
Q: Will this work on fiberglass hoods and aftermarket body panels?
Yes—adhesive bonds to fiberglass, metal, and painted surfaces. Automotive vinyl adheres to any smooth, clean, painted surface including: factory steel/aluminum hoods, fiberglass hoods (common on modified Trans Ams), aftermarket carbon fiber hoods, gel-coat fiberglass, and properly-prep painted plastics. Surface must be smooth (not heavily textured), clean (no wax, oils, dirt), and cured if recently painted (wait 30 days after fresh paint). Works equally well on original Pontiac sheet metal or modern replacement panels.

Q: What's the difference between vinyl decal and heat transfer options?
Vinyl = adhesive stickers for hard surfaces; Heat transfer = iron-on for fabric. **Vinyl decals** have adhesive backing for application to cars, toolboxes, windows, walls, etc. Durable outdoor use, removable, professional look. **Heat transfer vinyl (HTV)** is for fabric applications using heat press or iron: t-shirts, hoodies, hats, jackets, bags. No adhesive—bonds to fabric with heat/pressure. Choose vinyl for automotive/hard surface applications. Choose HTV for apparel and soft goods. Both available in same designs and colors.

Q: How do I remove an old screaming chicken decal before applying a new one?
Heat, patience, and adhesive remover. Process: (1) Heat old decal with heat gun or hair dryer (NOT torch—paint damage), (2) Peel slowly from one corner as adhesive softens, (3) If decal tears, reheat and continue, (4) Remove adhesive residue with Goo Gone, 3M adhesive remover, or isopropyl alcohol, (5) Clean thoroughly with car soap and alcohol before applying new decal. Old factory Pontiac decals (40+ years) may be brittle—extra heat and patience required. Professional decal removal services available at body shops if nervous about paint damage. Fresh paint should wait 30 days minimum before new decal application.
